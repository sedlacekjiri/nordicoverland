Campfire nights: how to set up camp in Iceland

24 September 2026

Iceland rewards the well-prepared camper. Weather changes fast, daylight shifts with the seasons and the best spots are often far from the nearest service. A little planning before you leave makes every evening around the fire easier.

Pick a good spot

Look for flat, firm ground with some shelter from the wind, and always check the local rules for where you may stay overnight. Park so the vehicle takes the brunt of the wind, and keep your awning side facing away from it.

Set up before dark

Open the roof tent, put up the awning and lay out your camp kitchen while there is still light. Everything is quicker with warm hands and a clear view of your gear.

Leave no trace

Pack out everything you bring, use existing fire rings or a camp stove, and stay on marked tracks. Iceland’s landscape recovers slowly, so the fewer marks you leave, the better.
